Format 16:9, ~2000×1125 px.

## 1. `rome-cannes.png` — Cannes, la nasse
> Codex illustration, bird's-eye view at dawn over a wide Apulian plain: two armies drawn as
> abstract geometric formations of tiny gold and dark-blue points — a Roman block of dense
> maniples in the centre being enveloped by a crescent-shaped Carthaginian line that curls
> around both flanks like a closing net; dust clouds, long shadows, a river (Aufidus) on one
> side. Night-blue palette (#050811, #0e1a2e), gold (#d6ac55) for Rome, terracotta (#b0613a)
> for Carthage. Cartographic, majestic, no visible bodies, no blood, no text, no logo.

## 2. `rome-cresus.png` — L'or de Crésus
> Codex illustration: the treasury of Sardis at night — heaps of small stamped gold and
> electrum coins bearing a lion and a bull, gold ingots and a river of golden sand (the
> Pactolus) flowing through a marble hall, an empty Lydian throne draped in purple in the
> background, and, faint on the wall, the ghostly silhouette of a funeral pyre. Gold
> (#d6ac55, #f3d98a) dominant on night-blue (#050811), purple (#8c3b4a) accents. Volumetric
> torchlight, fine grain, engraved grid. No people, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## 3. `rome-comices.png` — Les comices centuriates au Champ de Mars
> Codex illustration: the Campus Martius at dusk seen from above, a vast enclosure divided
> into wooden pens (the saepta) where crowds in togas queue in ordered columns towards narrow
> raised walkways (the pontes) leading to voting urns guarded by magistrates on a tribunal;
> the front pens, brightly lit in gold, are half-empty and finishing, the rear pens, in cold
> blue shadow, are packed and waiting. Night-blue (#0e1a2e) and gold (#d6ac55), purple
> (#8c3b4a) togas of magistrates. Faces not detailed. Engraved grid, volumetric light, no
> text, no logo, no watermark.

## 4. `rome-soie.png` — La Route de la soie
> Codex illustration: a caravan of camels and horses crossing a high desert pass at night
> under a vast starry sky, bolts of shimmering translucent silk and small crates of Roman
> glassware strapped to the animals; in the far distance on the left, faint marble columns
> and a Roman arch; on the far right, faint Chinese watchtowers and a rammed-earth wall with
> lanterns. Night-blue (#050811), gold (#d6ac55) firelight, purple (#8c3b4a) and terracotta
> (#b0613a) fabrics. Engraved grid, stars, volumetric haze, fine grain. Figures small and
> anonymous, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## 5. `rome-philosophes.png` — Penser sous les coups d'État
> Codex illustration: a quiet marble portico garden at night after a storm — overturned
> fasces and a broken sword lying on the wet steps in the foreground — and, further along
> the colonnade, a single lit reading room: a low table with scrolls, an oil lamp, a wax
> tablet and a stylus, a plain wooden stool, laurel and olive branches at the window. Gold
> lamp light (#d6ac55) against night-blue (#0e1a2e), laurel green (#4f7a63) accent. No
> people, no text, no logo, no watermark. Mood: inner freedom while the city burns.

## 6. `rome-siphon.png` — Le siphon de l'aqueduc
> Codex illustration, technical-plate mood: a Roman aqueduct arriving at the rim of a deep
> valley at night, water pouring from a stone header tank into a bundle of thick lead pipes
> that plunge down the slope, cross the valley floor on a low arched "venter" bridge, and
> climb the far side to a receiving tank; a cutaway detail showing the pressurised pipe
> section in cross-section with golden water; compasses and a plumb line in the corner.
> Night-blue (#050811), gold water (#f3d98a), terracotta (#b0613a) pipes, blue (#5b7fb5)
> highlights. Engraved grid, volumetric moonlight, fine grain. No people, no text, no logo.
